Employee Retention Intelligence & Attrition Early Warning System

Project Overview

Employee turnover can create significant challenges for organisations, including recruitment costs, loss of institutional knowledge, reduced productivity and disruption to business operations.

The Employee Retention Intelligence & Attrition Early Warning System is a data analytics and machine learning project designed to investigate the factors associated with employee attrition, identify potential retention risks and explore how organisations can use workforce data to support better employee retention decisions.

The project uses a simulated workforce dataset representing a Nigerian organisation, Nexus Workforce Solutions (NWS), with 2,000 employees and historical workforce records covering 2024–2025.

Project Objectives

- Analyse employee attrition patterns across departments, job roles and tenure groups.
- Identify workplace and organisational factors associated with employee turnover.
- Investigate career stagnation, compensation and employee engagement.
- Develop a six-month employee attrition prediction model.
- Explore potential employee retention interventions and their financial implications.
- Examine model performance and fairness across employee groups.

Dataset Description

The project uses three primary datasets:

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| Employee Master | Employee demographics, employment details, performance and career information. |
| Employee Exit Records | Historical employee resignation records and exit information. |
| Monthly Workforce History | Monthly employee observations, workplace experience indicators and six-month attrition targets. |

Dataset characteristics:
- 2,000 simulated employees.
- 155 simulated voluntary exits.
- 24,037 monthly workforce observations.
- 55 documented data fields.
